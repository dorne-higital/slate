1. Open **Theme** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Start from a palette**, pick a ready-made set of colours, or skip this step.
3. Under **Colours**, set your main colour, accent, dark, soft and text colours. Click a swatch or type a colour code.
4. Choose fonts for headings and text. The preview on the right updates as you go.
5. Press **Save theme**. Your whole site changes straight away.

![The Theme screen](shot:theme)

## Tips

- Pick a main colour with strong contrast against white, so buttons stay readable.
- Changed your mind? Press **Discard changes** before saving.
