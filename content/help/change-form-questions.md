1. Open **Forms**, choose the form and press **Edit form**.
2. Press **Add a question**. Type its label and choose the **Answer type**: short text, long text, email, phone or a choice.
3. For a choice, type the **Choices, one per line**.
4. Tick **Must be filled in** for questions people have to answer.
5. Change the **Button text** and the **Message after sending** if you like.
6. Press **Save form**, then **Back to replies**.

![Editing a form](shot:form-editor)

## Tips

- Shorter forms get more replies. Ask only what you need to give a quote or call back.
