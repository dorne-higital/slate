---
title: Add Calendly booking
summary: Let people book a visit or call on your site.
area: integrations
order: 3
related: whatsapp-button, add-a-block
popular: false
---
1. Copy your scheduling link from Calendly.
2. In Slate, open **Integrations** and the **Bookings** tab, then choose **Calendly**.
3. Paste your **Calendly link** and save.
4. Add a **Booking** block to the page where people should book (under **Get in touch** in the block library), then publish.
