1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under **People and your data**, press **Download a copy**.
3. You get one file with your pages, blog posts, menus, settings, form replies as spreadsheets, and your pictures.

## Tips

- Owners and admins can download, because it includes every form reply.
