1. When you add a page, choose its parent in **Put it under**. Pages under another page appear in a dropdown in the menu.
2. To change it later, open **Menus** in the sidebar.
3. Open the **Header** menu.
4. Use the arrows to move links up or down, or **Put … in a dropdown under the link above** to nest one.
5. Press **Save menu**.

## Tips

- Until you change your header menu by hand, Slate builds it from your pages automatically.
- See [Edit your menus](help:edit-your-menus) to add links to other websites or show a link as a button.
