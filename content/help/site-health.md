1. Open **Overview** and press **Site health** to open it.
2. The list shows what to improve, most important first: broken links, missing descriptions, big pictures and more.
3. Each item names the page and block. Press **Fix** to go straight there.
4. Fix it, publish or save, and the score goes up.
