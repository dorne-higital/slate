1. Open **Menus** in the sidebar.
2. Choose the menu: **Header**, **Footer** or **Footer small print**.
3. Press **Add a link**. Type its **Text** and choose where it **Goes to**: one of your pages or any web address.
4. Use the arrows to reorder links, or put one in a dropdown under the link above.
5. Tick **Show as a button** to make a link stand out, or **Open in a new tab** for other websites.
6. Press **Save menu**.

## Tips

- A button-style “Get a quote” link at the end of your header menu works well for most businesses.
