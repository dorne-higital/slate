1. Open **Theme** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Logo and icon**, press **Choose image** next to **Logo** and pick or upload your logo.
3. Press **Choose image** next to the icon and choose a square picture. It appears in browser tabs and bookmarks.
4. Press **Save theme**.

## Tips

- A logo with a transparent background (PNG or SVG) looks best on every header colour.
- Use a simple square icon, at least 64 × 64 pixels. A single letter or mark works better than your full logo.
- Change the logo size in **Layout**.
