Available on the Business and Studio plans.

1. Open **Integrations** in the sidebar.
2. Open the **Forms and email** tab.
3. Choose **Slack**, **Discord**, **Mailchimp**, **Brevo** or **Webhook** and press it.
4. Follow the steps on the card, such as pasting a Slack webhook address or connecting your Mailchimp list.
5. Press **Save**. New replies are sent there from now on.
