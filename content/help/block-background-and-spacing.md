Every block has the same section options, at the bottom of its settings.

1. Open the page in the editor and click the block.
2. Scroll to the bottom of its settings on the right and open **Section: background, spacing, fade-in**.
3. Choose a **Background**: page, soft, dark, your main colour or your accent colour.
4. Choose **Space above and below**: compact, normal or roomy.
5. Turn on **Fade in when scrolled to** if you’d like it to appear gently.
6. Optionally type a **Link name**, like “pricing”, so a menu can jump straight to this block.

![The section options for a block](shot:editor-section)

## Tips

- Text colours change automatically to stay readable on dark and coloured backgrounds.
- Alternate between page and soft backgrounds to separate sections on a long page.
