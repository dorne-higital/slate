1. Open **Billing** in the sidebar.
2. Press **Choose a plan** (or **Change plan** if you already have one).
3. Choose monthly or yearly, and the plan you want.
4. Pay securely by card. Your plan starts straight away.

![The Billing screen](shot:billing)

## Tips

- Yearly costs the same as ten months.
- Your invoices are listed under **Invoices**, ready to download as PDFs.
- To stop paying, press **Cancel subscription**. Your site stays online until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
