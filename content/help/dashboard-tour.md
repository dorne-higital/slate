The sidebar on the left takes you to every part of your site.

![The Overview screen](shot:overview)

1. Open **Overview** to see your live address, counts of pages, posts and form replies, and your site health score.
2. Use the **Content** group for words and pictures: **Pages**, **Blog**, **Projects**, **Forms**, **Media** and **Menus**.
3. Use the **Design** group for the look: **Theme** (colours, fonts, logo), **Layout** (header and footer) and **Blocks** (try every block).
4. Use the **Site** group for everything else: **SEO**, **Domains**, **Integrations**, **People**, **Settings**, **Billing** and **Support**.
5. To switch between sites, press **Switch** next to your site’s name at the top of the sidebar.

## Tips

- Every screen has a **Help with this page** link that opens the right part of this help centre.
- The number next to **Forms** shows new replies waiting for you.
