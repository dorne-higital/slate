1. In Google Analytics, create a property for your site and copy its **Measurement ID** (it starts with G-).
2. In Slate, open **Integrations** and choose **Google Analytics**.
3. Paste the **Measurement ID** and save.

## Tips

- Slate shows visitors a cookie choice automatically, as UK law needs for Google Analytics.
- Want simple numbers without cookies? Slate’s built-in visitor stats are already on your Overview.
