1. Open **Media** in the sidebar.
2. Press **Upload files** and choose one or more files. Or drag them onto the page.
3. They appear in your library, ready to choose from any **Choose image** button.

![The Media screen](shot:media)

## Tips

- Files can be up to 10 MB, but smaller is better: photos under 1 MB keep pages fast. Site health flags very large images.
- You can also upload straight from any **Choose image** button in the editor.
