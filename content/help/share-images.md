1. Open **SEO** and click a page.
2. Under **Share image**, press **Choose image** and pick a picture.
3. Press **Save changes**.

## Tips

- The best size is 1200 × 630 pixels.
- Set one under **Whole site** to use on every page that doesn’t have its own.
