1. Open **Layout** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Header style**, choose **Standard**, **Centred** or **Mega menu**.
3. Under **Header**, choose where it sits: **Above the page**, **See-through, on top of the first section**, or **Hidden**.
4. Pick the header and footer colours.
5. Press **Save layout**.

![The Layout screen](shot:layout)

## Tips

- See-through headers look great over a hero with a picture.
- To change the links themselves, see [Edit your menus](help:edit-your-menus).
