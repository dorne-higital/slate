1. Open the page in the editor and click the block.
2. Its settings appear on the right. Type to change words; use **Choose image** to pick a picture. Changes save as you type.
3. To copy the block, press the **two squares** icon in its toolbar. The copy appears underneath.
4. To delete it, press the **bin** icon in its toolbar.
5. Press **Publish** to make the changes live.

## Tips

- Deleted something by mistake? Press **Undo** at the top of the editor straight away.
- Lists inside a block, like services or reviews, have **Add** buttons at the bottom of their settings, and a cross to remove each item.
