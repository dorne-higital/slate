Blocks are the building pieces of every page: a heading, a list of services, a gallery, a form.

1. Open **Pages** and click the page you want to change. It opens in the editor.
2. Click the block you want the new one to go under. New blocks are added below the selected one.
3. In the left panel, open **Add a block**.
4. Open a group, such as **Show what you do** or **Proof and people**, or type in **Find a block**.
5. Click a block to add it, or drag it onto the page where you want it.
6. Type into the new block’s settings on the right, then press **Publish**.

![The block library in the editor](shot:editor-library)

## Tips

- Not sure what a block looks like? Press the **i** next to it, or try it first on the **Blocks** page.
- Every block follows your theme’s colours and fonts automatically.
