1. Open **SEO** and scroll to **Redirects**.
2. With **Add one**, type the **Old address**, like /old-page, and where to **Send people to**, like /services. Press **Add**.
3. Moving lots of pages? Choose **Paste a list** and paste one per line: old address, a space, then the new one.
4. Slate tells you about any lines it skipped and why.

![Adding a redirect](shot:redirects)

## Tips

- Redirects are permanent, so Google moves your ranking to the new page.
- Each redirect shows how many times it’s been used.
