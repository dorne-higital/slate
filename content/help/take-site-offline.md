1. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Offline or delete**, press **Take offline**.
3. Visitors now see your **Offline** page instead of your site.
4. Press **Put back online** when you’re ready.

![The Settings screen](shot:settings)

## Tips

- Design your Offline page from **Pages**, under **System pages**.
