1. Open the page in the editor from **Pages**.
2. At the top of the editor, press **Phone**. The page shrinks to phone width.
3. Scroll through and check the words and pictures fit well.
4. Press **Tablet** to check a mid-sized screen, or **Desktop** to go back.

![The editor showing a page at phone size](shot:editor-phone)

## Tips

- Most visitors to small business sites are on phones, so it’s worth checking every page this way.
- If a heading wraps awkwardly on a phone, a shorter heading usually fixes it.
