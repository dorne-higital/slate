1. Open **Forms** in the sidebar. New replies are marked unread.
2. Click a reply to read it.
3. Press **Reply by email** to answer from your own email.
4. Add a **Private note** if you want to remember something. Only people on your team can see it.

![A form reply open in the inbox](shot:forms)

## Tips

- Use the search and sorting at the top to find an older reply.
