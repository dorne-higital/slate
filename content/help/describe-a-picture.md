1. Open **Media** and click the picture.
2. Type a few words in **Describe the image**, like “Green armchair after reupholstery”.
3. Press **Save description**.
4. Pictures in blocks also have a **Describe the image** box in the block’s settings.

## Tips

- Leave it empty only for decorative pictures that add nothing to the words.
- Site health lists pictures on your pages that are missing a description.
