1. Open the page in the editor from **Pages**.
2. Click the block you want to move. A small toolbar appears above it.
3. Press the **up arrow** or **down arrow** in the toolbar to move it one place at a time.
4. Or drag it: hold the **dotted handle** at the left of the toolbar and drop the block where you want it.
5. Press **Publish** when the order looks right.

![A selected block with its toolbar](shot:editor-selected)

## Tips

- Made a mess? Press **Undo** (the curved arrow at the top) to step back.
- To reorder items inside a block, like services or reviews, use the arrows next to each item in the settings on the right.
