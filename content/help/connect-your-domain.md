1. Open **Domains** and press **Connect a domain**.
2. Type your domain, like yourbusiness.co.uk, and press **Connect**.
3. Slate shows the records to add. Log in to wherever you bought the domain and add them in its DNS settings.
4. Back in Slate, press **Check again**. It shows **Waiting for records** until they’re found, then sets up HTTPS for you.

![Connecting a domain](shot:domains-connect)

## Tips

- DNS changes can take from a few minutes to a few hours to be seen.
- Keep your existing email records as they are; you only add the ones Slate shows.
