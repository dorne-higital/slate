Slate keeps two versions of every page: your draft, which saves as you type, and the live version visitors see.

1. Open a page from **Pages**.
2. Make your changes. The top of the editor says **Draft saved. Not published yet.**
3. Press **Publish** at the top right when you’re happy. Visitors see the new version straight away.
4. Press **View live** to open the page on your site.

## Tips

- The **Pages** list shows **Unpublished changes** next to any page with edits visitors can’t see yet.
- Theme, layout, menus and SEO changes go live as soon as you save them; only page content waits for **Publish**.
- Every publish is kept. See [Undo a change or go back to an earlier version](help:undo-and-page-history).
