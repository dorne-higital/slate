Every page starts with a heading block, ready for you to add more.

1. Open **Pages** in the sidebar.
2. Press **New page** at the top right.
3. Type the **Page title**, for example “Prices”. This becomes the page’s address too (/prices).
4. If it belongs under another page, choose it in **Put it under**. Otherwise leave it as **Nothing (a main page)**.
5. Press **Create and open**. The new page opens in the editor.
6. Add blocks and words, then press **Publish** when you’re ready. Until then, visitors can’t see it.

![The Pages screen with the New page form open](shot:pages-new)

## Tips

- New pages are added to your header menu automatically, unless you’ve set up your menus by hand.
- To add a page under an existing one straight from the list, press the **+** next to that page.
- Not ready to share it? Leave it unpublished: it shows as a draft in your Pages list.
