1. Open the page in the editor from **Pages**.
2. With no block selected, the right panel shows **Page settings**.
3. To keep it out of Google, tick **Hide this page from search engines**. People can still visit it with the link.
4. To delete it, scroll to the bottom of **Page settings** and press **Delete this page…**, then **Delete page**.

## Tips

- If people or Google know the page’s address, add a redirect after deleting it, so they land somewhere useful. See [Add redirects](help:redirects).
- Your home page and system pages can’t be deleted.
