1. Open **SEO** in the sidebar.
2. Click a page in the list.
3. Type the **Title in search results** and the **Description** (one or two sentences about the page).
4. Watch the preview of how it could look on Google.
5. Press **Save changes**. It goes live straight away.

![The SEO screen](shot:seo)

## Tips

- Say what you do and where, like “Upholstery in Chester”.
- Under **Whole site**, set a description used for any page without one.
