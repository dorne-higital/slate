Every site starts with a contact form. Starter includes up to 3 forms, and Business and Studio up to 20.

1. Open **Forms** and press **New form** if you need another one. Give it a **Form name**.
2. Open the page you want it on in the editor.
3. Under **Add a block**, open **Get in touch** and click **Form**.
4. In the block’s settings, choose which form to show.
5. Press **Publish**.

## Tips

- Replies arrive by email and in your **Forms** inbox. Change the email address in **Settings** under **Email for form replies**.
- Forms are protected from spam automatically, with no puzzles for your visitors.
