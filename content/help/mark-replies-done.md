1. Open a reply from **Forms**.
2. Press **Mark as done**. It moves out of **To do**.
3. Use the **To do**, **Done** and **All** tabs to switch between them.
4. Press **Mark as unread** if you want to come back to one later.

## Tips

- Site health on your Overview reminds you about replies left waiting more than a week.
