Signing up takes a couple of minutes, and you don’t need a card for the free trial.

1. Go to [slatecms.co.uk/signup](/signup).
2. Fill in **Your name**, **Email** and a **Password**.
3. Under **What’s your business called?**, type your business name. It’s used for your site’s name and free address.
4. Choose **Where’s your business based?**
5. Pick the starting layout closest to your business, such as a trade, salon or shop. It gives you pages with blocks already in place. Choose blank if none fit.
6. You land on your **Overview**. Your site is already live on its free address, shown at the top.

## Tips

- Nothing is fixed: you can rename, add or remove any page the layout gives you.
- Check your inbox for the email asking you to confirm your address. You’ll need it confirmed before buying a domain.
