1. Open the post from **Blog**.
2. Press **Schedule** instead of **Publish now**.
3. Choose the date and time under **Publish on**.
4. Press **Schedule post**. It shows as **Scheduled** in your Blog list until then.

## Tips

- Changed your mind? Open the post and press **Move to drafts**.
