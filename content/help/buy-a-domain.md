1. Open **Domains** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Buy a domain**, type the name you’d like and press **Search**.
3. Pick an available one from the list and press **Buy**. You’ll pay securely by card.
4. Slate registers it in your name and connects it to your site with HTTPS. This usually takes a few minutes.

![Domain search results](shot:domains-search)

## Tips

- You need to have confirmed your email address before buying.
- .co.uk is the most familiar ending for UK businesses.
