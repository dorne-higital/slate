1. Open **People** in the sidebar.
2. Optionally type **Their email** and **Name**.
3. Choose their **Role**: owner, admin, editor or viewer. See **What each role can do** below the form.
4. Press **Make invite link** and send them the link. They sign in or create an account and join your site.

![The People screen](shot:people)

## Tips

- How many people you can add depends on your plan.
