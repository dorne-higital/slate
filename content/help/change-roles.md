1. Open **People**.
2. Next to the person, choose a new role, or press **Remove** to take away their access.

## Roles

- **Owner**: everything, including billing, domains and deleting the site.
- **Admin**: everything except billing and deleting the site.
- **Editor**: pages, blog, menus, forms and replies.
- **Viewer**: can look at everything but not change it.
