For domains bought through Slate.

1. Open **Domains** and find your domain.
2. Press **Email forwarding**.
3. Type the **Address**, like “hello”, and where to **Send to**, like your Gmail address.
4. Save it. Send a test email to check it arrives.

## Tips

- Add as many addresses as you like: hello@, bookings@, accounts@.
- To reply from your business address, add it as a “send mail as” address in Gmail or Outlook.
