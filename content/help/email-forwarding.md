Email forwarding gives you a professional address for free, without a new inbox to check. Say your domain is daveplumbing.co.uk and you already use Gmail: customers email **hello@daveplumbing.co.uk**, and it lands in your Gmail like any other email.

It’s for domains you bought through Slate. It takes about a minute.

1. Open **Domains** in the sidebar.
2. Under your domain, press **Email forwarding**.
3. In the first box, type the start of the new address, like **hello**. The rest (@yourbusiness.co.uk) is filled in for you.
4. In the second box, type the inbox it should go to, like your Gmail address.
5. Press **Add**. The address shows as “Check … and press the link from Cloudflare”.
6. Open that inbox. You’ll have an email from Cloudflare asking you to confirm. Press the link in it. (Not there after a few minutes? Check spam.)
7. Back in Slate, the address now says **Working**. Send an email to it from another account to check it arrives.

## Tips

- Add as many as you like, such as hello@, bookings@ and accounts@. Each can go to a different inbox.
- Confirming is only needed once per inbox. Add more addresses to the same inbox and they work straight away.
- To stop one, press **Remove** next to it.
- Replies you send come from your own address (like your Gmail one). If you’d like replies to come from hello@ too, Gmail and Outlook can do it with their “send mail as” setting. It takes a few more steps, so search your email provider’s help for “send mail as”.
- Connected a domain you bought somewhere else? Forwarding is set up where you bought it, not in Slate. Most domain companies offer it free.
