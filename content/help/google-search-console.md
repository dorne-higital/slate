1. Go to search.google.com/search-console and add your site as a **URL prefix** property.
2. Choose the **HTML tag** method and copy it.
3. In Slate, open **Integrations**, choose **Google Search Console** and paste it into **Verification code**.
4. Save, then press **Verify** in Search Console.
5. In Search Console, open **Sitemaps** and submit sitemap.xml.

## Tips

- If your domain is already verified with Google another way, you don’t need this. Site health spots it.
