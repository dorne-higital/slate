1. Open **Pages** and scroll to **System pages**.
2. Press **Edit** next to **Page not found**.
3. Change it like any other page: add blocks, words and a button back to your home page.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Tips

- The **Offline** system page is what visitors see while your site is offline. See [Take your site offline](help:take-site-offline).
- System pages are hidden from search engines and menus automatically.
