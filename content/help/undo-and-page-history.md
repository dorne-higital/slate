1. While editing, press **Undo** (the curved arrow at the top of the editor) to step back one change at a time.
2. To go back further, press **History** at the top of the editor. You’ll see every time the page was published, who published it and a short summary.
3. Press **Restore** next to the version you want. It goes back into your draft, so nothing changes on your site yet.
4. Check it, then press **Publish** to make it live.

![The History window](shot:editor-history)

## Tips

- Slate keeps the last 30 published versions of every page.
- Restoring doesn’t delete anything: the version you replaced stays in the history too.
